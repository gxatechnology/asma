Taxonomy Architecture

Harmonizing IFRS, US GAAP & Iranian Standards

Financial translation cannot rely on literal word replacement. Differences between IFRS (IASB), US GAAP, and Iranian National Accounting Standards (INAS / استانداردهای حسابداری ایران) require conceptual reconciliation.

sync_alt Dual Codification Mapping

Every row and chart of accounts line-item is matched to its accredited equivalent under the Iranian Audit Organization (سازمان حسابرسی) lexicons.

Framework 01: IFRS to INAS Convergence Standardized

Addressing fair value measurement disclosures, inflation adjustments, and statutory reserves specified by Iranian Commercial Code articles 140 & 238.

Framework 02: Numerical Formatting & Persian Calender Bilateral

Seamless synchronization between Solar Hijri fiscal cycles (ended Esfand 29 / 12/29) and Gregorian accounting periods without computational displacement.

Framework 03: Multi-Currency & Dual Exchange Systems FX Compliance

Clear labeling of NIMA rate, Central Bank official parity, and free market translation rates to prevent ambiguity in foreign subsidiary consolidation.
