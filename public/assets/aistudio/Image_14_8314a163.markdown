## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-prestige aesthetic tailored for elite English-to-Persian translation, literary localization, and cross-cultural communication consultancy. The visual narrative combines modern luxury, quiet editorial restraint, and international scholarly rigor.

The atmosphere evokes the tactile elegance of archival parchment, heavy-stock literary folios, and museum-grade typography. Rather than flashy commercialism, the design communicates linguistic mastery, academic precision, and elevated diplomacy.

### Style Directives
- **Modern Luxury Editorial:** Expansive margins, asymmetrical balancing, and strict typographic hierarchy.
- **Bilingual Symmetry:** Perfectly weighted pairings between Latin and Persian letterforms, ensuring equivalent visual hierarchy and emotional tone across LTR and RTL scripts.
- **Calm Prestige:** Subtle contrast shifts over saturated fills, relying on fine 1px hairline rules, luminous gold accents, and deep charcoal body text to communicate seriousness and quality.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in historic manuscript materials refined for high-resolution contemporary screens.

- **Ivory White (`#F8F6F1`):** Canvas background. Delivers a soft, low-glare surface reminiscent of vellum.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved exclusively for elevated cards, modal sheets, and active layered containers.
- **Champagne Beige (`#EAE2D6`):** Secondary structural surfaces, subtle card containers, divider rules, and quiet hover fills.
- **Muted Gold (`#B79656`):** The primary signature tone. Applied to brand monograms, primary callouts, subtle hairline accents, and interactive focal points.
- **Dark Charcoal (`#22272B`):** Authoritative neutral tone used for primary typography, luxury dark-mode callouts, and grounding baseline elements.
- **Soft Sage Green (`#788A78`):** Restrained secondary accent for editorial badges, verified certifications, footnote notations, and serene highlights.

### Semantic Mapping
- **Surface Canvas:** `#F8F6F1`
- **Surface Raised:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Subtle:** `#EAE2D6`
- **Text Primary:** `#22272B`
- **Text Muted:** `rgba(34, 39, 43, 0.65)`
- **Border Default:** `#EAE2D6`
- **Border Accent:** `rgba(183, 150, 86, 0.25)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges high-end Western editorial design with Middle Eastern literary tradition. 

- **English Headlines:** Set in **Playfair Display**, evoking classic serif bookplates, literary journals, and diplomatic missives. Titles use optical kerning and delicate leading.
- **English Body & Interface:** Set in **Inter**, selected for its neutral geometry and high legibility across fine data points, credentials, and documentation.
- **Persian Localization (RTL):** Pair with **Vazirmatn** for all Persian copy. Headings use weights 600/700 with a 1.45 line-height ratio, while body text uses weights 400/500 with a 1.7 line-height ratio to preserve the natural balance of Arabic-script ascenders and descenders.

All labels and meta tags in Latin script feature subtle uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an editorial fluid grid that maximizes generous breathing room, reminiscent of wide book margins.

### Grid Configuration
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max content width 1320px, `margin-lg` (5rem) margins, and `gutter-lg` (2.5rem) column spacing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin-md` (3rem) margins, and `gutter` (1.5rem) column spacing.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, `margin` (1.25rem) margins, and `space-sm` (0.75rem) gutters.

### Rhythm & Vertical Spacing
- Sections transition with expansive vertical padding (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) to preserve calm authority.
- Text blocks maintain an optimal reading measure of 60 to 72 characters per line.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh drop shadows and heavy multi-layered skeumorphism. Depth is achieved through tonal layering, fine hairlines, and warm, diffused ambient shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Ivory White (`#F8F6F1`) backdrop with no shadow. Structural dividers use `1px solid #EAE2D6`.
- **Level 1 (Card / Resting Surface):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Champagne Beige (`#EAE2D6`) containers with `1px solid rgba(183, 150, 86, 0.18)` outline and ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(34, 39, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Elevated Menus):** Applied to active cards, localization previews, and floating navigation bars: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(34, 39, 43, 0.07)`, paired with an accented border `rgba(183, 150, 86, 0.35)`.
- **Golden Glow (Interactive Elements):** Pill action buttons feature a signature luminous halo on hover: `0 0 24px rgba(183, 150, 86, 0.28)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs an intentional architectural contrast: softly rounded structural containers juxtaposed against fully curved pill-shaped interactive triggers.

- **Containers & Editorial Panels:** Soft corners (`roundedness: 3` baseline mapping to `1rem` on desktop cards, `1.5rem` on featured showcase units) creating a warm, approachable frame for text.
- **Buttons, Tags & Filter Pills:** Full capsule geometry (`9999px` / pill-shaped) to signal effortless tactility and modern finesse.
- **Hairline Borders:** Exact 1px line weights across all cards, tags, and dividers to ensure fine-gauge editorial craft.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background in Muted Gold (`#B79656`), text in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), fully rounded pill radius (`9999px`), padding `12px 32px`. Hover initiates a subtle scale shift (`1.02`), tone brightening (`#C4A466`), and an ambient gold glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(183, 150, 86, 0.28)`).
- **Secondary Editorial Pill:** Transparent background, `1px solid #22272B` border, text in Dark Charcoal (`#22272B`). On hover, fills with Dark Charcoal (`#22272B`) and flips text to Ivory White (`#F8F6F1`).
- **Text Link:** Set in Dark Charcoal (`#22272B`) with a fine gold underline (`#B79656`) offset by 4px.

### Cards & Case Studies
- Elevated on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px Champagne border (`#EAE2D6`).
- Internal padding of `space-lg` (`2rem`).
- Bilingual comparison cards feature a central vertical hairline divider with dual-pane layout: English on the left, Persian on the right.

### Input Fields & Consult Forms
- Background in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #EAE2D6` border, `12px` border radius, padding `14px 18px`.
- Typography: Inter 15px.
- Focus State: Border transitions to Muted Gold (`#B79656`) with a subtle ring shadow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(183, 150, 86, 0.15)`).

### Chips & Domain Badges
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`), background in Champagne Beige (`#EAE2D6`) or translucent Sage (`rgba(120, 138, 120, 0.12)`).
- Text in 11px uppercase Inter (`label-sm`), Dark Charcoal (`#22272B`) or Soft Sage Green (`#788A78`).

### Bilingual Comparison Viewer
- A dedicated consultancy component displaying parallel texts. 
- Features language identifier pills (`EN` / `FA`), synchronized scroll markers, and subtle gold indicators highlighting localized cultural nuances.
