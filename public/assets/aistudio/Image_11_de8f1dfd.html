Inquiries & Protocols

Frequently Answered Questions

Essential details regarding turnaround schedules, engagement workflows, and translation methodologies.

Standard velocity ranges from 2,000 to 2,500 words per business day for dense financial or corporate legal texts to maintain thorough terminology cross-checking. For urgent tenders or regulatory deadlines, an expedited priority workflow is available upon consultation.
All engagements can be preceded by a binding Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Source files are stored in encrypted environments, never fed into public machine-translation tools or shared with unauthorized third parties. Complete data purge is executed upon milestone acceptance.
No unassisted machine translation is delivered. Every passage is translated and contextualized by Asma Parnianfard. Professional Computer-Assisted Translation (CAT) tools like SDL Trados are employed strictly for corporate terminology consistency and glossary enforcement across long-form folios.
All major formats are supported: Microsoft Word (.docx), Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx), Adobe InDesign interchange (.idml), PDF documents, PowerPoint (.pptx), XML/JSON localization files, and raw text files. Formatting structures and table alignments are meticulously preserved.
